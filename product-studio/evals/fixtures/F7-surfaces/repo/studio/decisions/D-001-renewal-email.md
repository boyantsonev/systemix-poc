# The renewal reminder is a plain email a week before renewal

## Context
Support tickets about surprise renewals came mostly from teams who never opened the in-app notice.

## Decision
One plain email seven days before renewal: the plan, the amount, the date, a link to Settings to
cancel, and a one-click unsubscribe. It opens with the person's name.

## Why
People read email about money; they skip banners.
