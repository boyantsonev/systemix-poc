# Let a person invite a teammate to a notebook in under a minute

## Success criteria
- A new admin invites a teammate from inside a notebook without opening Settings.
- The invite reaches the teammate as one email with one link.

## Kill if
- People keep inviting from Settings anyway after two weeks.

## Context
- Asked as: "inviting someone should not need Settings".
- Today the only invite path is Settings, Members. (source: src/features/settings/SettingsScreen.tsx)
