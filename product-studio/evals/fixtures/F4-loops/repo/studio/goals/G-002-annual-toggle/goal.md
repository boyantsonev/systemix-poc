# Show the yearly price first on the pricing page

## Success criteria
- The pricing page opens on yearly prices with the monthly price one tap away.
- Every price names its period on the same line.

## Kill if
- Monthly sign-ups fall by more than a fifth with no rise in yearly sign-ups.

## Context
- Builds on L-001: people who saw the yearly price first chose yearly about twice as often. (source: studio/LEARNINGS.md)
