# Show the yearly price before the monthly one

## Context
In five observed sessions on the old pricing page, people who saw the yearly price first chose it
twice as often as people who saw the monthly price first.

## Decision
The yearly price comes first wherever both are shown. The period is always on the same line.

## Why
It matches what people chose when nobody nudged them, and it keeps the renewal visible.
