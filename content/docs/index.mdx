---
title: What is Systemix?
---

Systemix is the design system agent for AI-native products. It sets up your design system, keeps it from rotting, and runs your design sprints, inside Claude Code. **You approve every step.** Everything it knows lives as plain files in your repo.

## Start in two minutes

1. **Install the plugin.** In Claude Code:

   ```
   /plugin marketplace add boyantsonev/systemix-poc
   /plugin install systemix@systemix
   ```

2. **Run `/systemix:score`** in your repo. It reads your code on your machine and prints a score out of 100, per area. Nothing is sent.
3. **Fix what costs points.** Do it yourself, or buy the quick report, which lists each fix, biggest first.

No Claude Code? Run `npx @getsystemix/cli audit report --dirs src` in your repo.

## What it does

- **Deploy:** `/systemix:design-interview` drafts your design system with you; `/systemix:design-audit` proposes one from the code you have.
- **Keep:** `/systemix:score`, `/systemix:drift-report` and `/systemix:readiness-audit` find raw colours, one-off sizes and duplicate components.
- **Sprint (beta):** `/systemix:goal` turns one sentence into a goal, then pictures, a prototype and a release, with a gate at every step.
