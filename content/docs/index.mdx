---
title: What is Systemix?
---

Systemix is the design system agent for AI-native products. It sets up your design system, keeps it from rotting, and runs your design sprints, inside Claude Code. **You approve every step.** Everything it knows lives as plain files in your repo.

## Start in two minutes

1. **Install the plugin.** In Claude Code:

   ```
   /plugin marketplace add boyantsonev/systemix-poc
   /plugin install systemix@systemix
   ```

2. **Run `/systemix:setup`** in your repo. It proposes your design system and its rules (`design/rules.md`) from your code; you approve each file. Nothing is sent.
3. **Run `/systemix:check`** on a folder of UI. It lists what breaks a rule, biggest first, with the fix for each.

No Claude Code? Run `npx @getsystemix/cli audit report --dirs src` in your repo.

## What it does

- **Deploy:** `/systemix:setup` builds your design system and its rules (`design/rules.md`) from the code you have, from a short interview, or from a Claude Design output.
- **Keep:** `/systemix:brief` gives the rules before you build; `/systemix:check` checks the work against them, with `--score`, `--drift` and `--deep` for the score, the drift from your tokens and the long audit.
- **Sprint (beta):** `/systemix:loop open` turns one sentence into a goal, then pictures, a prototype and a release, with a gate at every step.
