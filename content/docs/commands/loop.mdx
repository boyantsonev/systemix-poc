Takes a goal from one sentence to shipped and measured, through the
[product and build loops](/docs/concepts/loops), stopping at every gate for a person.
**Only you run this command**: a gate is a ruling, and the agent never rules.

```
/systemix:loop [status]
/systemix:loop open <one sentence>
/systemix:loop next [G-NNN]
/systemix:loop gate approve|rework|drop [G-NNN] [note]
/systemix:loop decide <loop.step>
```

| Subcommand | What it does |
|---|---|
| `status` (or nothing) | Where every goal is and whose gate it waits on. Sets the loops up in a repo that has none |
| `open <sentence>` | Opens a goal: the reading of your sentence, its evals, an empty decision log. Stops at the Evals gate |
| `next [G-NNN]` | Runs the agent's steps until the next person or gate, then stops and says who must look |
| `gate approve\|rework\|drop` | Records your ruling, word for word, and moves the goal on |
| `decide <loop.step>` | The decision brief before a gate, so whoever holds the role, or their deputy, decides from the same facts. Never decides |

## A goal is a folder

`/systemix:loop open` writes `design/goals/G-NNN-<slug>/`:

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `goal.md` | The sentence as typed, how the agent reads it, for whom, risks, the questions for the gate. Frontmatter holds the state: `step` and `waiting` |
| `evals.md` | Five to twelve evals: what must be true, how it is checked, the bar, when it is answered |
| `decisions.md` | One line per ruling, in the person's own words, with their role |
| `artifacts.md` | The pictures, from the design step on |
| `plan.md` | The build plan and what was decided while building |

`systemix loops check` refuses anything malformed, a ruling by someone who doesn't hold the
gate's role, and any ruling made by the agent.

## How a turn goes

1. You type `/systemix:loop open <sentence>`. The agent writes the goal and its evals, and stops.
2. You rule: `/systemix:loop gate approve G-007`, or `approve` with changes in the note, or `rework`
   or `drop`.
3. `/systemix:loop next G-007` runs the agent's steps (pictures, the check until it passes, the plan,
   the prototype, the code) and stops at the next gate.
4. Repeat until the release is measured and the lessons are written to `experiments/LEARNINGS.md`.
