# Ads name the price with its period and never call it cheap

## Context
An ad that said "$8" without a period drew complaints from people who expected a yearly price.

## Decision
Every ad names the price with its period. We never call the product cheap.

## Why
Clear prices build trust; "cheap" undersells the paid plan.
