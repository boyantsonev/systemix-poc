# Business — what the founder knows

What Systemix charges, what it costs, and who pays. A decision here is judged by
whether it moves money or a commitment signal, and whether it can be undone.

## What we know
- Systemix is solo-founder work; every phase must be runnable by one person plus agents `docs/feature/ai-native-ds-kit-beta/discover/wave-decisions.md § Constraints`
- Price ladder: free plugin and score, €9.99 quick report, €499 full audit in 24 h, design sprints `wave-decisions.md DSC-16`, `src/lib/pricing.ts`
- Design sprint: a fixed 3-day sprint at €990 is the price to test (founder took the recommendation, 2026-10-31) `wave-decisions.md DSC-20`
- Payments run on Lemon Squeezy license keys; the service checks only store and product, so the price can change without a deploy `src/lib/report-access.ts`
- Lemon Squeezy fees are about 5% + $0.50 per sale (third-party figure) `docs/feature/ai-native-ds-kit-beta/funnel-plan.md § 2`
- Never sell UI code; charge for audit, implementation, wiring and the loop `wave-decisions.md DSC-5`
- Public budgets seen for this kind of work: $100–$720 `wave-decisions.md DSC-11`
## Numbers
- Revenue: €0 (no product on sale yet) `funnel-plan.md`
- Paid reports, audits, sprints sold: 0
- Interviews done for the G1 gate: 0 of 5 `wave-decisions.md § Phase tracker`
- Cost per free score: one short server call; not measured yet in money
- Audit review time: 45–90 min, an estimate, not measured `wave-decisions.md DSC-16`
## Open bets
- The €9.99 report is the cheapest real commitment signal for G1 `wave-decisions.md DSC-17`
- Audits, not reports, carry revenue: €1,000 a month is about 2 audits or 125 reports `docs/feature/ai-native-ds-kit-beta/launch-summary.html`
- A €990 fixed sprint sells where €4.5k for two weeks did not get a hearing `wave-decisions.md DSC-20`

## Questions the expert would ask
- Who pays, from which budget, and what did they pay for last time?
- What does one more unit cost us, in money and in founder hours?
- Is this a commitment signal (money moved) or stated intent?
- Can we undo it if it is wrong, and how fast?
- What do we stop doing to make room for it?
